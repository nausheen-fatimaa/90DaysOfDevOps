# My 90-Day DevOps Learning Plan 🚀

## 1. About Me – Current Level

I am an MBA graduate transitioning into the DevOps and Cloud Engineering domain. I have completed DevOps training and gained foundational knowledge of Linux, AWS, Docker, Jenkins, Kubernetes, Terraform, Ansible, Prometheus and Grafana.

I understand the fundamentals of these technologies, but I want to strengthen my practical implementation skills, troubleshooting abilities and confidence in solving real-world infrastructure problems independently.

## 2. My Understanding of DevOps & Cloud Engineering

DevOps is a combination of practices, tools and culture that brings development and operations together to automate software delivery, improve collaboration and ensure reliable deployments.

Cloud Engineering involves designing, deploying, managing and maintaining scalable infrastructure using cloud platforms such as AWS.

I believe DevOps and Cloud Engineering are essential for building reliable, automated, scalable and highly available applications.

## 3. Why Am I Starting This Journey?

I am starting this 90-day challenge to transform my theoretical knowledge into practical industry-level skills.

My objective is to become confident in implementing DevOps workflows, managing cloud infrastructure, troubleshooting technical issues and handling real-world deployment scenarios.

I want to become a job-ready candidate for entry-level DevOps Engineer or Cloud Engineer positions.

## 4. My Three Goals for the Next 90 Days

1. **Practical Expertise:** Strengthen my Linux, AWS, Docker, Kubernetes and CI/CD implementation skills through hands-on projects.
2. **Real-World Projects:** Build and document independent DevOps projects involving automation, infrastructure provisioning, application deployment and monitoring.
3. **Career Readiness:** Become confident in technical interviews, troubleshooting scenarios and explaining my project implementations.

## 5. Three Core Skills I Want to Build

- **Linux & Troubleshooting:** Debug system issues, analyze logs, manage processes and resolve infrastructure problems independently.
- **CI/CD & Automation:** Build reliable Jenkins pipelines and automate application build, testing and deployment workflows.
- **Cloud & Kubernetes:** Deploy applications, manage AWS infrastructure and troubleshoot Kubernetes workloads.

## 6. My Weekly Time Commitment

- Monday – Friday: 3 hours daily.
- Saturday: 4 hours of practical implementation and revision.
- Sunday: Weekly revision and progress evaluation.

**Weekly commitment: Approximately 19 hours.**

## 7. My Consistency Strategy

- Practise every day and document what I learn.
- Focus on hands-on implementation rather than memorizing commands.
- Troubleshoot errors before looking for solutions.
- Maintain my GitHub repository with daily progress.
- Revise previously learned concepts regularly.
- Track my progress and complete weekly self-assessments.

## 8. My Final Commitment

My goal is not simply to complete 90 days of learning. My goal is to become a confident, independent and job-ready DevOps and Cloud professional.

I understand that consistency, patience and practical experience are more important than perfection.

**90 Days. Consistent Learning. Real-World Implementation. Career Growth.**

Let's build it, break it, fix it and learn! 🚀
